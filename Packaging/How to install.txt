AIRLOOM - putting it on another computer
========================================

1. Copy the "Airloom" folder (or unzip "Airloom for Windows.zip") somewhere on
   the computer, e.g. the Desktop or Documents. There's nothing to install:
   Airloom.exe runs on its own on Windows 10 or 11.

2. Double-click Airloom.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC",
   click "More info" then "Run anyway" (it appears because the app isn't
   signed by a publisher - it's your own build).
   Tip: right-click Airloom.exe > "Show more options" > "Send to" >
   "Desktop (create shortcut)" for a shortcut.

3. Set it up once on that computer:
   - Plug in your audio interface (install its driver first, e.g. Focusrite
     Control for the Scarlett) and pick it in Audio Settings.
   - Plug in the MidiMix and the Fire - they're found automatically.
   - Install the plug-ins your sets use (Vital, Serum, Graillon...) from their
     makers. Then press + INSTRUMENT > Scan for plug-ins once.

4. Bring your sets over: on the old computer use Set > Export set as one file,
   copy the .liverigpack across, then on this computer Set > Open set and pick
   it. It unpacks into Music\Airloom Sets and opens ready to play. The pack
   includes "Plug-ins needed.txt" listing what to install.

Settings for this computer (audio device, MIDI, the last session) are kept in
%APPDATA%\LiveRig. Recorded loops are saved in %APPDATA%\LiveRig\Recordings
until you save or export the set. (The app was called Live Rig before; it
keeps that folder, so nothing needs moving.)

To update Airloom later, just replace Airloom.exe with the new one.
Coming from Live Rig: delete the old Live Rig.exe and its desktop shortcut,
and make a new shortcut to Airloom.exe - all your settings carry over.
